# Method (draft 1)

We model the flow speed v with dv/dt = k (1 - v)^alpha(F), where the exponent depends on the forcing F (formula v2).

The equation is solved with the FSF solver v0.2 (adaptive step size).

As a cross-check we also use a lattice discretisation on a 64 x 64 grid.
